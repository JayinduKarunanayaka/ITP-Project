# Medication and Adherence Tracking System

A comprehensive medication management platform developed to help patients and caregivers manage medications, track adherence, monitor health information, and receive medication reminders.

## Project Overview

The Medication and Adherence Tracking System is designed to improve medication adherence by providing reminder notifications, inventory management, prescription storage, health tracking, and adherence reporting.

The system supports both patients and caregivers, allowing caregivers to manage multiple patients from a single account.

---

## Features

### User Management
- User Registration and Login
- Role-based Access Control
  - Patient
  - Caregiver
- Password Recovery and Verification

### Medical Information Management
- Medication Management
- Allergy Tracking
- BMI Tracking
- Medical Records Management
- Health Notes

### Time Allocation & Reminders
- Schedule Medication Reminders
- Mobile Push Notifications
- Medication Intake Tracking
- Reminder History

### Inventory Management
- Medication Stock Tracking
- Expiry Date Monitoring
- Prescription Management
- Automatic Stock Updates

### Appointment Management
- Appointment Scheduling
- Appointment Notes
- Doctor Information Management

### Tracking & Reporting
- Medication Adherence Tracking
- Missed Dose Monitoring
- Adherence Percentage Calculation
- Dashboard Analytics
- Activity Logs

### Feedback System
- User Feedback Submission
- Rating System
